We design from the joint out — shoulders, hips, knees — so the fabric moves before your body has to fight it.

How it's made

Four-way stretch

Every fabric we use stretches on both grains, so a shirt or a pair of trousers moves with a reach or a lunge and returns to shape after.

Gusseted seams

Underarms, crotch and knees get an extra panel of fabric at the seam, the same construction used in performance wear, so nothing rips at the stress point.

Articulated cut

Sleeves and knees are cut with a slight pre-bend rather than straight, matching the natural resting angle of an arm or a leg.

Flat, low-bulk seams

Seams are bonded or flat-felled where they'd otherwise sit under a bag strap or a waistband, so nothing chafes on a long day.
